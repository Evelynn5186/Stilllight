## Journal Screens

### Journal - Gather a little light
> Your collection of moments. Tap "Gather a little light" to revisit a memory with Gemini's warmth.

---

### Journal - light Outcome
> Gemini reflects your past moment: "Simple moments can still mean something." — AI-powered comfort.

---

## Settings Screens

### Setting (Main)
> Customize your Luminary experience. Safety features, reminders, and pause options — all in one place.

---

### Setting (Sleep Mode Enabled)
> Pause check-ins when you need space. The light waits patiently, no alerts triggered.

---

### Safety (Overview)
> Safety first. Set emergency contacts and missed check-in alerts to stay connected.

---

### Safety - Emergency Contact
> Add someone who cares. If you go quiet, Luminary can reach out on your behalf.

---

### Safety - Missed Check-ins
> Customize when to alert your contact. A gentle safety net, not surveillance.

---

### Reminder
> Gentle nudges to check in. Set your preferred time and frequency — no pressure, just presence.

---

### Account (Profile)
> Your profile. Manage your account details and sign out when needed.

---
